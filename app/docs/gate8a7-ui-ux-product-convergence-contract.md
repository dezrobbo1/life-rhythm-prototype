# Gate 8A7 — UI/UX Product Convergence Contract

Status: implemented surface contract for PR from `f30eb4ea271e4e92a73c015778a0d9de76fb51e1`.

## Copy standard

Ordinary copy names the fact, consequence and available choice: what Life Rhythm knows, what it can privately do when feasible, what the user chose, what changed, and what remains safe. It never treats a blank calendar gap as capacity or an internal placement as an external calendar event. Advanced details may retain exact technical vocabulary where the distinction is needed for data or backup inspection. Avoid shame, scores, hidden psychological claims, and promises of guaranteed placement.
